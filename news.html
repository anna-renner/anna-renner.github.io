We have a new article in Accounts of Chemical Research:

Renner, A. C.; Raju, A.; Subramanian, H.; Sibi, M. P. Fluxional Nitrogen in Play: A Strategy for Enhancing Stereoselectivity in Asymmetric Catalysis. Acc. Chem. Res. [Online early access]. DOI: 10.1021/acs.accounts.6c00141.

Please take a look if you're interested in reading about applications of fluxional chirality at nitrogen in asymmetric catalysis.

Many thanks and congratulations to Prof. Mukund Sibi, Aleena Raju, and Prof. Hari Subramanian!
