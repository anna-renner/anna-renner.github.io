News

May 4, 2026

I am very pleased to announce that our article "Fluxional Nitrogen in Play: A Strategy for Enhancing Stereoselectivity in Asymmetric Catalysis" has been published in Accounts of Chemical Research and is now available to read online. Many thanks to Prof. Mukund Sibi and to Aleena Raju and Prof. Hariharaputhiran Subramanian for their contributions to this work.

Renner, A. C.; Raju, A.; Subramanian, H.; Sibi, M. P. Fluxional Nitrogen in Play: A Strategy for Enhancing Stereoselectivity in Asymmetric Catalysis. Acc. Chem. Res. [Online early access]. DOI: 10.1021/acs.accounts.6c00141.

This Account summarizes the Sibi group's research on the use of nitrogen-centered fluxional chirality in asymmetric catalysis. We hope the article will be an informative and valuable resource for others interested in asymmetric catalysis and the fascinating subject of fluxional chirality.
